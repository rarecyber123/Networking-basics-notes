
## 📱 Mobile Device Connectivity 

> Mobile devices give us the freedom to connect, work, and learn from anywhere without being tied to a physical location .

### Three key wireless technologies:
- Wi-Fi
- Cellular Data
- Bluetooth.

---

🗝️ Important Terms to Know:

*SSID* (Service Set Identifier)

> The unique name assigned to a wireless network.

*Passphrase*

> The wireless password used to secure and authenticate your connection.

*Plaintext*

> Unencrypted data (never send passwords or sensitive information over plaintext!).

*VPN (Virtual Private Network)* 

>A secure, encrypted tunnel used to protect sensitive data over public Wi-Fi.
