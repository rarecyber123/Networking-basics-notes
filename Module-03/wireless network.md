
## Types of Wireless Networks
 > Mobile devices rely on different layers of wireless technology depending on coverage area and distance requirements:

   - Cellular Networks (GSM / 4G / 5G): Wide Area Networks (WANs) provided by cellular carriers. They handle long-distance communication (voice calls, SMS, mobile data) using cellular towers and base stations.

   - Cell Phone Interactions: Smartphones automatically switch between available connections (e.g., jumping from cellular data to a saved Wi-Fi network) to maintain connectivity while optimizing data usage and battery life.

 ## Key Short-Range & Satellite Technologies (Important Terms)
 
> Here are the essential wireless technologies every network tech should know, ordered from global coverage down to micro-range connectivity:

### 🌐 GPS (Global Positioning System)

   **What it does** : Uses a network of global satellites to calculate a device’s physical location.

   **Key Detail** : Highly accurate—typically pinpoints location within 10 meters.

   **Direction** : Read-only / Receiver-only (your phone receives signals from satellites to calculate coordinates).

---

### 📶 Wi-Fi (Wireless Fidelity)

   **What it does** : Connects devices to local area networks (LANs) and the internet using wireless access points (APs) or routers.

 **Hotspot** : A specific geographic area where a Wi-Fi signal is available for public or private access.

**Use Case** : Ideal for high-speed data transfer over medium distances (homes, offices, cafes).

---

## 🎧 Bluetooth

**What it does** : A low-power, short-range wireless technology designed to replace wired cables for peripheral accessories.

**Key Detail** : Supports simultaneous connection of multiple devices for both voice and data transmission.

**Use Case** : Connecting smartwatches, wireless headphones, speakers, mice, and keyboards over small local networks.

---

## 💳 NFC (Near Field Communication)

*What it does* : Very short-range wireless communication operating via electromagnetic fields over a few centimeters.

*Key Detail*: Highly secure due to the required physical proximity.

*Use Case*: Contactless mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Wallet) and quick tap-to-pair device interactions.
