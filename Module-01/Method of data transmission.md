
# 📡 Common Methods of Data Transmission

## 📌 Overview
Data ko bit patterns (0s aur 1s) mein convert karne ke baad, ise network par signals ke zariye destination tak bheja jata hai. 

**Media** woh physical medium hota hai jis par signals travel karte hain (jaise copper wires, optical cables, ya air).

---

## ⚡ 3 Main Transmission Methods

### 1. Electrical Signals (Copper Wires)
* **How it works:** Data ko **pulses of electricity** (voltage levels) ke roop mein bheja jata hai.
* **Media Used:** Copper cables (e.g., Twisted pair / Ethernet cables).
* **Usage:** Commonly used in home networks, small offices, and desktop PCs.

### 2. Optical Signals (Fiber-Optic Cables)
* **How it works:** Electrical signals ko **pulses of light** mein convert karke transmit kiya jata hai.
* **Media Used:** Fiber-optic cables.
* **Usage:** Long-distance connections, backbone networks, and large enterprise infrastructure (higher bandwidth & speed).

### 3. Wireless Signals (Air/Atmosphere)
* **How it works:** Data ko **electromagnetic waves** (radio waves, microwaves, infrared) ke zariye travel karwaya jata hai.
* **Media Used:** Air (Atmosphere).
* **Usage:** Wi-Fi networks, cellular/mobile data, and satellite communications.

---

## 📊 Quick Comparison

| Transmission Method | Signal Type | Physical Media | Primary Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Electrical** | Pulses of Electricity | Copper Wire | Local LAN, Home Networks |
| **Optical** | Pulses of Light | Fiber-Optic Cable | High-speed & Long Distance |
| **Wireless** | Radio Waves / Microwaves | Air | Wi-Fi, Mobile Networks |

---

> 💡 **Key Takeaway:** Network signals can be converted multiple times between electrical, optical, and wireless forms as media changes along the path from source to destination.
