

## Connected Home Devices

The modern home network consists of smart technology commonly categorized under the **Internet of Things (IoT)**.

### Common Smart Home Categories:

| Category | Examples | Primary Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Smart Appliances** | Refrigerators, ovens, washing machines | Remote monitoring, energy management |
| **Smart Home Security** | Security cameras, smart locks, doorbells | Remote video feeds, access control |
| **Environmental Controls** | Thermostats, smart lighting, smart plugs | Automation, remote temperature & power control |
| **Smart Assistants** | Amazon Alexa, Google Nest, Apple HomePod | Voice control hub for smart home automation |

---




### 3. Enterprise & Industrial (IIoT)
* **Smart Meters:** Digital utility meters (electricity/water) that report real-time usage directly to utility companies.
* **RFID Tags & Sensors:** Used in supply chain logistics to track inventory and packages in real-time.

---

> **Key Takeaway:** End devices are no longer just desktop computers and laptops. Modern networks must securely support **Mobile**, **IoT Home**, and **Industrial Connected Devices**.
