# 🌍 What is a Network?

> A **network** is a group of devices connected together so they can communicate and share resources.

These devices may communicate through:

-🔌 Wired connections (Ethernet)

- 📶 Wireless connections (Wi-Fi)

A network can be as small as two computers or as large as the Internet.


## 🧠 Analogy

City roads carry people → Networks carry data.

# 📌 Main Purposes of Networking


Networking allows us to:

✅ Share files

✅ Share printers

✅ Access the Internet

✅ Communicate instantly

✅ Work together remotely

✅ Store data centrally

## 🧠 Interview Corner
**Q1. What is a network?**  
A collection of connected devices that communicate and share resources.

**Q2. Name three shared resources.**  
- Files  
- Printers  
- Internet  

**Q3. Is Wi-Fi the Internet?**  
No. Wi-Fi is just a wireless connection method.
