
## ISP (Internet Service Provider)

> Ek organization ya company jo users, homes, aur businesses ko Internet se connect hone ki facility provide karti hai.

## 🌐 ISP Connectivity Options

 An **Internet Service Provider (ISP)** provides the link between home/small office networks and the global internet. ISPs connect hierarchically to form the **Internet Backbone**—a high-speed global network primarily connected via underground and undersea **fiber-optic cables**.

--- 

### 1. ISP Services
* **ISP (Internet Service Provider):** Yeh woh company hoti hai jo aapke ghar ya office network ko internet se connect karti hai.
* **Internet Backbone:** High-speed fiber-optic cables ka global network jo dunya bhar ke ISPs ko aapas me jorta hai (cables zameen aur samandar ke neeche hoti hain).
* **Extra ISP Services:** Email accounts, web hosting, network storage, security/backup, voice over IP (VoIP), technical support.

---

### 2. ISP Connections

* **Direct Connection (Single PC):** Modem ke zariye PC ko direct ISP se connect karna (unsafe hota hai, is liye use nahi karna chahiye).
  
* **Router Connection (Multiple PCs):** Integrated Router (jisme Switch, AP, aur Router hotay hain) ko modem se connect karke secure internet chalana.

---

### 3. Common Connection Types

* **Cable:**
  * Coaxial cable (Cable TV network) par data chalta hai.
  * Always-on aur high bandwidth connection hai.
  * Cable modem TV signal aur internet data ko alag karta hai.

* **DSL (Digital Subscriber Line):**
  * Landline telephone line par chalta hai (always-on connection).
  * Line ko 3 channels me baanta jata hai: **Voice**, **Download**, aur **Upload**.
  * Distance jitna zyada hoga central office se, speed utni kam hogi.

* **Cellular:**
  * Mobile towers (3G/4G/5G) ke zariye internet connection.
  * Travel karne walon ya door-draz ilaqon ke liye behtar hai.
  * Bandwidth usually **metered** hoti hai (data limit cross karne par extra charges lagte hain).

* **Satellite:**
  * Cable aur DSL na hone par best option hai.
  * Clear **Line of Sight** (azad rasta) chahiye hoti hai satellite dish ke liye.
  * Setup aur installation expensive hoti hai.

* **Dial-Up Telephone:**
  * Normal phone line aur dial-up modem ka istemal hota hai.
  * Boht low bandwidth hoti hai aur connection ke waqt phone line busy rehti hai.

---

### Key Terms to Remember
* **ISP:** Internet access dene wali organization.
* **Internet Backbone:** Continental aur undersea fiber cables ka main global network.
* **Integrated Router:** Ek hi box jisme Router, Switch, Wireless Access Point, aur Modem ke features hotay hain.
* **Coaxial Cable:** TV aur internet signal ek sath carry karne wali copper cable.
* **DSL Modem:** Phone line ki frequencies ko data aur voice me separate karne wala device.
* **Line of Sight:** Dish antenna aur space satellite ke beech bina kisi rukawat ke saaf rasta.
* **Metered Bandwidth:** Limited data plan jisme limit se upar data use karne par extra bill aata hai.


### 🔌 Connectivity Options Comparison

| Connection Type | Medium / Infrastructure | Key Features & Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cable** | Coaxial Cable (Cable TV Network) | High bandwidth, "always-on" connection. Uses a **Cable Modem** to separate internet data from TV signals. |
| **DSL** | Landline Telephone Line | "Always-on" high-speed access. Splits line into 3 channels (Voice, Download, Upload). Performance degrades with distance from central office. |
| **Cellular** | Mobile Towers (3G / 4G / 5G) | Ideal for mobile access or remote areas. Often subject to **metered bandwidth** (data limits/charges). |
| **Satellite** | Satellite Dish & RF Signals | Requires a clear **Line of Sight** to space satellite. Great for rural areas; higher setup costs & latency. |
| **Dial-Up** | Analog Telephone Line | Low bandwidth, legacy option. Requires dialing an access number; occupies phone line during use. |
