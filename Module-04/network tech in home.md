
# 1. Wireless Technologies 

Aam gharon mein istemal hone wali wireless technologies unlicensed 2.4 GHz aur 5 GHz frequency bands par kaam karti hain:

## Unlicensed Electromagnetic Spectrum

> Spectrum ke kuch hisse bina kisi permit ya licence ke use kiye ja sakte hain.

## Bluetooth Technology

- Frequency Band: 2.4 GHz band istemal karta hai.

- Features: Low-speed aur short-range communication ke liye design kiya gaya hai.

- One-to-Many Communication: Ek sath multiple devices se communicate kar sakta hai.
  
- Use Cases: Computer peripherals (wireless mice, keyboards, printers) aur audio streaming (speakers, headphones) ke liye sabse behtareen hai.

## Modern Wireless LAN (Wi-Fi / IEEE 802.11):

- Frequency Bands: 2.4 GHz aur 5 GHz bands.

- Features: Bluetooth ke muqable high power level par transmit karta hai, jis se iski range aur data throughput bohot zyaada hoti hai.

# 2. Wired Network Technologies

> Aisa data traffic jo shared network par wireless interference bardasht nahi kar sakta, uske liye wired connections istemal hotey hain:

## Ethernet Protocol:

> Wired LAN connections ke liye sabse aam taur par istemal hone wala protocol suite hai.

## Ethernet Patch Cable (UTP)

> Unshielded Twisted Pair (UTP) cables mein RJ-45 connectors lage hotey hain.

## Powerline Technology:

> Agar ghar ke andar UTP wiring (Ethernet wall jacks) na ho, toh powerline devices ke zariye ghar ki bijli ki taaron (electrical wiring) ko wired network ke liye istemal kiya ja sakta hai.
