
# LAN (Local Area Network)

- ### Basic Definition:

   Aisa network jo ek hi administrative control ke andar ho.

- ### Characteristics:

   Pehle yeh sirf ek location tak mehdood hota tha, ab isme multiple buildings ke hundreds of hosts shamil ho sakte hain. Isme zyada tar Ethernet ya Wireless protocols use hote hain aur data speed fast hoti hai.

- ### Intranet:

   Kisi organization ka private LAN jo sirf unke authorized logon ya employees ke liye access-able hota hai.

  ---

 # Single Local Segment 

- ### How it works :

  Tamam devices ek hi broadcast domain me hoti hain aur aapas me ARP ke zariye communicate karti hain.

- ### Fayde:

  Simpler design, kam kharcha, direct fast communication, aur devices ko aapas me connect karna aasan hota hai.

- ### Nuksan:

   Ek hi broadcast domain hone ki waja se network par traffic zyada hota hai, speed slow ho sakti hai, aur QoS ya Security apply karna mushkil hota hai.
