
# 1. IPv6 ki Zaroorat Kyun Pari? (The Need for IPv6)

### IPv4 Khatam Ho Raha Hai:
 
IPv4 ke paas lagbhag 4.3 billion addresses hain, jo duniya bhar ki nayi devices, smartphones aur Internet of Things (IoT) ke hisab se bohot kam hain.

### - IPv6 Ka Size: 

IPv6 128-bit address use karta hai. Isme kul 340 undecillion (340 ke aage 36 zeroes!) addresses milte hain—jo kabhi khatam hone wale nahi lagte.

### - RIR Exhaustion: 

Duniya ke lagbhag 5 me se 4 Regional Internet Registries (RIRs) ke paas ab naye IPv4 addresses khatam ho chuke hain.

### - NAT Ki Limitations: 

NAT (Network Address Translation) ne IPv4 ko zinda toh rakha, par yeh connection me latency (delay) laata hai aur peer-to-peer applications ke liye masla banta hai.

### - Advanced Features: 

IPv6 sirf bada address nahi deta, balki isme ICMPv6 jaisi behtar technologies hain jo auto-configuration aur address resolution me madad karti hain.
